## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The home page earns its thesis; the room inherits it. Home is one physical object, two doors differentiated by material (filled yellow = Buat Room, chalk outline = Gabung), and a right column that is a *working demonstration* of the mechanism — three names, a circled total, `Bima → Sari / Bima → Dika`, "Dua transfer saja — sudah lunas semua." That receipt is the value proposition rendered as an artifact. 8/10. The room carries the material system (mono totals, circled total, paper bills with pins and torn edges, the inline tear-off delete) but its two most important panels — "Ringkasan Saldo" and "Siapa Transfer ke Siapa" — are two symmetrical `rounded-2xl` bordered panels in `lg:grid-cols-2`, i.e. the dashboard card the design system's own Don't list forbids. 6.5/10. Combined 7.5/10.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect --json` over `app/page.tsx`, `app/r/[code]/page.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx`, `app/error.tsx`, `app/not-found.tsx` → **exit 0, zero findings**. The detector saw nothing wrong with a page that loses 132px of money content on a 390px phone. This is the clearest possible statement of the detector's ceiling: it reads markup patterns, not layout outcomes.

**Visual overlays:** Injection succeeded (`document.title` mutable, injected `<script>` executed), live-server ran on port 8400, overlay rendered, console reported **6 anti-patterns**: `unbalanced-heading` on the receipt title, `straggler` on "(1 porsi)" wrapping alone, and 4 × `nested-cards`. Treat 3–4 of those as false positives: "panel inside the board" and "note pinned on the board" are the committed design language, not card-in-card SaaS nesting. Only the `nested-cards` hit on `#gabung-room-panel` (dashed box inside the board, duplicating the header CTA) points at a real hierarchy problem.

## Overall Impression

The home page is the best thing here and would survive a portfolio review. The room page is themed rather than designed around its own protagonist, and it has a hard layout defect that deletes the one number the product exists to deliver — on the phone, at 390px, for the person who just opened a WhatsApp link. The craft budget is inverted: the most beautiful interaction (inline tear-off delete) is visible only to admins, while the most broken one is visible to everyone who isn't.

## What's Working

1. **The synthetic receipt is a mechanism demo, not marketing** (`app/page.tsx:60–84`). It proves the greedy settlement before asking for anything. No fabricated social proof anywhere in the app.
2. **Money is visually non-negotiable.** `.tnum` mono everywhere, `formatSignedRupiah` emits explicit `+`/`-` so color is never the only signal, and yellow means money-in or the one primary action. `DESIGN.md`'s Figures-Are-Mono and Money-Is-Yellow rules are actually enforced, not aspirational.
3. **The inline delete confirmation** (`BillList.tsx:28–105`) — real `alertdialog`, Escape, focus-to-confirm, focus-restore, quoted description and amount, "saldo dihitung ulang dan tagihan tidak bisa dikembalikan". Exactly the reassurance a money app used by friends needs.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Mobile room: balance and settlement amounts are clipped offscreen — 132px of content, unreachable
**What:** At 390×844 and 360×740 on `/r/KPR7PPPCKC`, the "Ringkasan Saldo" and "Siapa Transfer ke Siapa" sections measure **484.9px wide** inside a 314px column, and the board's `overflow-hidden` ancestor (`div.board-grain`, width 346, right edge 368) crops them. `+Rp 87.667`, `-Rp 19.167`, `-Rp 68.500`, `Rp 0`, and both transfer amounts render past x=368 and are permanently invisible. `scrollWidth` stays 390, so there is no scrollbar to rescue them.
**Root cause (measured, not inferred):** `BalanceSummary`'s `<ul className="grid gap-3 sm:grid-cols-2">` is a single-column grid. The implicit column track sizes to the **max-content of the widest item**, and the `<li>` grid items have default `min-width: auto` while the amount span is `shrink-0`. One long member name — "Muhammad Rizky Pratama Wijayakusuma Hidayatullah" — sets the track to 442.9px, and **every** row inherits that width, which is why all four rows clip even though only one name is long.
**Proven fix:** setting `min-width: 0` on those `<li>` elements drops the track to 272px, rows to 272px, and puts the amount right edge at **316 < 368**. One token on the row, no structural change. The room-empty case is clean because no member name is long enough to blow the track — this is data-dependent, which is why it shipped.
**Why it matters:** PRODUCT Principle 3 ("satu layar menjawab 'aku harus transfer berapa ke siapa'") and Principle 2 fail simultaneously, on the surface and device where they matter most.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`

### [P1] The room never identifies the viewer
**What:** `BalanceSummary.tsx:49–74` renders members in arbitrary order and `SettlementPanel.tsx:52–70` renders routes with no marker. `currentMemberId` is already computed in `app/r/[code]/page.tsx:35` and passed to `AddBillForm`, but not to either money panel.
**Why it matters:** the user must scan a mono list to find their own name, then cross-reference a second list. At 4 members that is friction; at the product's stated 15-member ceiling it is the exact manual matching the app exists to eliminate.
**Fix:** pass `currentMemberId` into both panels, sort the viewer first, append a chalk "(kamu)", and lead the settlement panel with one line: "Kamu transfer Rp 68.500 ke Dika." No new data required.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] Cold entry shows a stranger a total that isn't theirs
**What:** For non-members, `RoomHeader`'s circled "Total tagihan: Rp 187.000" renders above `JoinRoomPanel`. The first yellow, circled, emphasised object on the page is a number the visitor has no claim on, and "Gabung" appears twice (header pill → anchor, then the panel's own button).
**Why it matters:** the first gesture a cold visitor needs is identity, not arithmetic. It also weakens The Money Is Yellow Rule: on the home, yellow = the thing to press; here, yellow = a number you can't act on.
**Fix:** for `showJoinCta`, render the join panel before the circled total; drop the header's duplicate "Gabung" pill in that state and let the panel own the action.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P2] No preview of per-person amounts before saving a bill
**What:** In SHARES mode the form validates only "porsi ≥ 1 integer". `src/lib/split.ts` floors and charges the remainder to the payer, so the arithmetic is safe — Assessment A's "sum silently over/under-allocates" is **wrong** and I am dropping it — but the user still cannot see the resulting Rp figures until after saving.
**Why it matters:** "tidak ada yang merasa dirugikan pembulatan" is a stated success criterion; the rounding rule is invisible until it has already happened.
**Fix:** a live footer in SHARES mode showing the computed split per participant, sourced from the same pure function the API uses.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Header action type and weight are inconsistent
**What:** `CopyLinkButton` renders `secondaryButtonClass` with an override of `text-sm` (`CopyLinkButton.tsx:31`) while every other use is `text-base`; the room's "Gabung" is a small pill next to the home's full filled block. On mobile the two stack in a column, primary action smaller than the panel title beneath it.
**Fix:** remove the `text-sm` override; on `<sm` make the join action full-width.
**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (phone, one-handed, noisy warung):** opens `/r/KPR7PPPCKC` at 390px, scrolls to "Ringkasan Saldo", sees four bordered rows with initials and names and **no numbers at all** — the amounts are past the clip. Concludes the app is broken and screenshots it into the group chat. Only the sticky "Tambah Tagihan" capsule is in thumb range, and its handler depends on `document.querySelector("#tambah-tagihan [aria-controls]")` (`StickyAddBillBar.tsx:17–19`) — if that toggle is absent the bar silently does nothing.

**Jordan (first-timer arriving from a WhatsApp link):** sees "Gabung" twice before he has decided whether he belongs here, a "Salin link" button before he knows what the room is, and a circled total that implies he owes something he hasn't agreed to. He cannot tell whether joining is a commitment. No exit, no rename, no "you can leave later" reassurance.

**Alex (the admin who creates the room):** the best craft in the product — the tear-off delete — is his alone, and he never sees the defect because his own session shows the same clipped panel. He will add bills confidently (default-all-selected, self-as-payer, digit-only amount) and then wonder why nobody in the group replies.

## Minor Observations

- Tap targets: **zero violations** at 360/390/768/1440 — every interactive element ≥44×44. Sub-12px text: **zero**. Both are genuinely clean.
- `formatRupiah` grouping (`Rp 187.000`) and `.tnum` are correct and consistent.
- Paper and board share the same mono family, so the "printed vs handwritten" material contrast is weaker than `DESIGN.md` claims; a heavier mono weight for paper only would restore it.
- `paper-torn`'s `mask-size: 200px 12px` stretches to ~160px on small chips, softening the teeth (visible on bill #2 in the mobile capture).
- `errorTextClass` uses a text-shadow for board contrast but is shared into paper contexts; no current error renders on paper, so it is latent rather than live.
- After all transfers are paid the page rests at "Tidak ada yang perlu transfer" — there is no "selesai" moment, so the journey ends on a shrug rather than a close.
- The sticky capsule is the one persistent element on mobile that could belong to any app; it is a stock CTA in board colours.
